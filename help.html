Help & Guide

Everything you need to know about using Streetly Science Hub

🚀 Getting started

1
Sign in with your school Google account — use your @thestreetlyacademy.co.uk email at the login page.
2
Select your class — a prompt will appear asking you to choose your class group (e.g. 10S4). This helps your teacher track your progress.
3
Pick a subject — choose Biology, Chemistry, or Physics from the home screen.
4
Choose a module — select the topic your class is currently studying. Modules are organised by unit.
5
Start revising! — use the activity tabs (Revise, Flashcards, Words, Cloze, Scribble, Mini Mock, Paper) to earn XP and track your progress.

⭐ XP and Brain Tiers

Every topic has an XP score from 0 to 100. As you complete activities you earn XP, which raises your score and unlocks higher Brain Tiers. Your tier shows how well you know each topic.

🎮 Activities explained

Each module has 8 tabs. Here's what each one does and how much XP you can earn:

🎓
Learn
Read through the topic notes. No XP — but this is where you build the understanding you need for everything else.
No XP
📖
Revise
A 3-question quiz. Answer questions from each topic in the module. 3 XP per correct answer. Once a topic hits 10 XP, it stops earning here.
⚡ Up to 9 XP · Max 10 per topic
🃏
Flashcards
5 flashcards per round. Flip each card and mark yourself. 1 XP for every card you get right. Keeps going until a topic reaches 35 XP.
⚡ 1 XP per card · Max 35 per topic
🔤
Words
Drag keywords to their definitions. 1 XP for every correct word. Stops earning once a topic reaches 35 XP.
⚡ 1 XP per word · Max 35 per topic
🧩
Cloze
Fill in the blanks in a passage of text. Up to 5 XP per round depending on how many blanks you fill correctly. Stops at 35 XP per topic.
⚡ Up to 5 XP · Max 35 per topic
✍️
Scribble
Write everything you can remember about the topic from memory, then check it against the mark scheme. XP scales with how much you remembered. Stops at 50 XP per topic.
⚡ Up to 10 XP · Max 50 per topic
✏️
Mini Mock
Exam-style questions on individual topics. Marks are scaled: 7/10 = 7 XP, 10/10 = 15 XP etc. No XP cap — you can take it up to 100.
⚡ Up to 15 XP · No cap
📝
Paper
A full past paper covering multiple topics. Results are split by topic and XP is awarded for each. The best way to push topics to Expert and Legend.
⚡ Up to 15 XP per topic · No cap

📊 My Progress

Click 📊 My Progress in the top navigation bar to see a summary of your XP across all subjects and modules. You can see which topics are your strongest and which need more work.

ℹ️ Your teacher can also see your progress — this helps them know which topics to focus on in class.

🚀 Why Streetly Science Hub?

Most revision platforms are glorified quiz apps — students answer questions, get a score, and move on. Streetly Science Hub is different. It is built around three principles that have a strong evidence base in cognitive science: metacognition, spaced retrieval, and adaptive challenge. These aren't add-ons — they are woven into every part of how the platform works.

📖 The EEF rates metacognitive strategies as having an average impact of +7 months of additional progress — one of the highest-impact, lowest-cost interventions available to schools.

✨ What makes it different

🧠
Metacognition built in
Every activity is designed to make students think about their own thinking. Scribble asks students to write from memory before checking answers — forcing retrieval rather than recognition. Flashcards require self-marking. The XP system makes the gap between "I've seen this" and "I actually know this" visible.
📉
XP decay (forgetting curve)
Topic XP scores decay automatically when students stop practising — mirroring how memory actually works. A student who reaches Expert tier but doesn't revisit the topic will drift back down. This creates a genuine need to return to revision and prevents students from ticking a topic off as "done" when they haven't truly retained it. Higher tiers decay faster: Legends lose 0.3 XP/day, Rookies lose 1.2 XP/day (after a 7-day grace period).
🤖
AI marking
Mini Mock and Paper responses are marked by Claude AI against the AQA mark scheme. Unlike simple keyword matching, the AI understands scientific reasoning — it can award partial credit, identify misconceptions, and give specific written feedback, just as a teacher would. Students get instant, personalised guidance without waiting for a book to be marked.
🪜
Scaffolded difficulty
Activities are ordered from lower- to higher-order thinking: Learn → Revise → Flashcards → Words → Cloze → Scribble → Mini Mock → Paper. XP caps on early activities push students forward rather than letting them farm easy points indefinitely. The only way to reach Legend tier is through exam-style practice.
📡
Real-time teacher insight
The Teacher Portal shows live XP scores and Brain Tiers for every student in your classes — no manual data collection, no waiting for a test. You can see at a glance which topics a class is struggling with and plan intervention accordingly, before the assessment rather than after.
📅
Curriculum-aware
Module cards reflect each student's curriculum timeline — modules they haven't reached yet are flagged as future content, and approaching assessment dates appear as colour-coded warnings (🔴 within 7 days, 🟠 within 14 days). Students revise what's relevant now, not whatever they happen to click on.
🎮
Intrinsic motivation
The XP and Brain Tier system turns revision into a game students want to play — but the game is carefully designed so the only way to score points is to genuinely learn the content. There are no shortcuts: you cannot grind flashcards to Legend tier. Engagement and learning are aligned, not in conflict.
🔒
Safe, school-owned, free
Built specifically for The Streetly Academy. No adverts, no upsells, no third-party tracking, no premium tier. Students sign in with their existing school Google account — no new passwords. Data is stored in EU Google servers under UK GDPR. Unlike commercial platforms, the school has full control and visibility of the data.

🏫 Getting the most out of it in lessons

  • Starters: Ask students to complete a Revise quiz on last lesson's topic — takes 3 minutes, instant formative data.
  • Homework: Set a specific activity tab as homework using the Assignments feature — students see it flagged when they log in.
  • Pre-assessment: The week before a test, check the Teacher Portal — students still showing Rookie or Apprentice on a topic need targeted support.
  • Scribble as brain dumps: Use the Scribble activity in class as a low-stakes brain dump before introducing new content. It surfaces prior knowledge and misconceptions.
  • Mini Mock feedback: AI-marked Mini Mock responses are great for modelling exam technique — pull up the feedback on screen and discuss it as a class.

👨‍🏫 The Teacher Portal

Access the Teacher Portal from the 👨‍🏫 Teacher badge in the navigation bar. From here you can view live XP and Brain Tier data for all students in your classes, create assignments, and monitor progress over time.

ℹ️ If you don't have access to the Teacher Portal, ask the site administrator (n.clifford@thestreetlyacademy.co.uk) to grant you teacher access.

📊 Reading student progress

In the Teacher Portal, select a class to see all students in that group. Click any student to expand their full topic-by-topic XP breakdown. Brain Tier colours give an at-a-glance summary:

  • ⚪ Not started — hasn't attempted this topic yet
  • 🔴 Rookie (1–33) — early stages
  • 🟠 Apprentice (34–55) — getting there
  • 🟡 Scholar (56–75) — solid understanding
  • 🟢 Expert (76–89) — strong grasp
  • ⭐ Legend (90–100) — ready for exam conditions

A 📉 indicator next to a tier means the score is actively decaying due to inactivity — a useful prompt to encourage students to revisit that topic before it slips further.

📋 Creating assignments

1
Go to the Teacher Portal and click Assignments.
2
Click New Assignment. Fill in the title, subject, module, and which activity tab to direct students to.
3
Select the class groups to assign it to, set a due date, and optionally add a note.
4
Click Create. Students in the selected classes will see the assignment flagged on the relevant module card.

📅 Setting assessment dates

Assessment dates appear as coloured flags on module cards for students in the relevant class (🔴 within 7 days, 🟠 within 14 days, 📅 further out).

1
Go to Admin Panel → Curriculum Schedule.
2
Select the class group and subject, then add the assessment date and which modules it covers.
3
Students in that class will immediately see the flag on the relevant module cards when they log in.

🏫 Admin panel

The Admin Panel (🏫 Admin badge in the nav) is available to admin and superadmin roles. From here you can:

  • Accounts tab — grant or remove teacher access for staff members
  • Students tab — view all registered students, their class, when they joined, and when they last logged in
  • Curriculum Schedule tab — manage assessment dates across all class groups
⚠️ Data deletion: Students who haven't logged in for over 1 year are flagged with ⚠️ in the Students tab and a Delete button appears. Run a sweep each September after results day to stay GDPR-compliant.

After 7 days of inactivity on a topic, the score begins to fall. The rate depends on the tier — higher tiers decay more slowly (Legend: −0.3 XP/day, Expert: −0.5, Scholar: −0.8, Apprentice: −1.0, Rookie: −1.2). This mirrors the shape of the Ebbinghaus forgetting curve: recent learning decays faster than consolidated knowledge. Students who maintain regular practice will hold their tier; those who leave a topic untouched will drift down. Once a student has held Legend tier for 28 days, their score is marked as Retained — it no longer decays.
