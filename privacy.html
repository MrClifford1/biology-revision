Privacy Notice

This notice explains what information Streetly Science Hub collects when you use it, why we collect it, and how it is kept safe. We've written it in plain English so it's easy to understand.

What information we collect

We only collect the information needed to make the revision tool work and to let your teacher track your progress.

Information Where it comes from Why we need it
Your name Your Google account (when you sign in) So your teacher can identify your progress in the dashboard
Your school email address Your Google account (when you sign in) To link your account to The Streetly Academy and your class
Your profile photo Your Google account (optional) Displayed on your profile only — not shared with teachers
Your class You select it when you first sign in So your teacher can see progress for their specific class
Your revision activity and scores Generated as you use the site To show your Brain XP progress and help your teacher support you

💡 We do not collect any sensitive personal data (such as medical information, ethnicity, or financial details). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

⚖️

Our legal basis for using your data

Under UK GDPR, we need a lawful reason to process your personal data. We rely on legitimate interests — specifically, providing an educational revision tool that supports student learning and allows teachers to monitor and improve progress.

Because most users are under 18, we take extra care to limit what we collect to only what is strictly necessary for this educational purpose.

🗓️

How long we keep your data

Your data is kept for as long as you are actively using the service. If your account has not been logged into for more than one year, your account and all associated data will be deleted. This means that data is automatically removed approximately one year after you finish your GCSEs (since you will no longer log in).

You can also request deletion of your data at any time — see the "Your rights" section below.

👀

Who can see your information

  • You — you can always see your own scores and progress.
  • Your science teachers at The Streetly Academy — they can see your name, class, and revision scores to help support your learning.
  • School administrators — can access data for safeguarding or school improvement purposes.
  • No one else — your data is never sold, shared with third parties for marketing, or used for any purpose outside of this school revision tool.
☁️

Third-party services we use

Streetly Science Hub is built using the following external services. These act as data processors — they store and process data on our behalf but cannot use it for their own purposes.

Service What it does Privacy information
Google Firebase (Google LLC) Stores your account data, scores and revision progress securely in the cloud Data is stored on EU/UK servers. Google is certified under the UK adequacy framework.
Google Sign-In (Google LLC) Handles your login using your school Google account We only request your name, email and profile photo — nothing else from your Google account.
Netlify Hosts the website and serves the pages you visit Standard web server logs (IP address, browser type) are retained briefly for security purposes.
Anthropic Claude AI Powers the AI marking in the Scribble and Mini Mock tabs Your written answers are sent to Claude for marking. Answers are not stored or used to train AI models.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

👁️ Right to access

You can ask to see a copy of all the personal data we hold about you.

✏️ Right to correction

You can ask us to correct any information that is inaccurate or incomplete.

🗑️ Right to deletion

You can ask us to delete your account and all associated data at any time.

⏸️ Right to restriction

You can ask us to limit how we use your data while a concern is being resolved.

📦 Right to portability

You can ask for a copy of your data in a portable format.

🚫 Right to object

You can object to us processing your data based on legitimate interests.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in the "Who we are" section above. We will respond within 30 days.

📣

If you have a concern about how we handle your data, please contact us first and we will do our best to resolve it.
